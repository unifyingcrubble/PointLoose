## 1. What PointLoose does

PointLoose lets a phone send mouse, keyboard, clipboard and presentation commands
to a Windows PC over your local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth Low Energy. While it is
running, any device holding the current connection link (Wi-Fi) or pairing code
(Bluetooth) can control that PC.
